Help & Guest Guidelines

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out timings for cottages?

Check-in is at 12:00 PM and check-out is at 10:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out is subject to availability and can be arranged in advance via our WhatsApp reservation desk.

Is swimming pool access included with Day Outing and Cottage bookings?

Yes! Swimming pool access with reclining sun-loungers is complimentary for overnight cottage guests and Day Outing package holders. Proper nylon swimwear is required.

What culinary options are offered at Borsha Restaurant?

Borsha Restaurant specializes in authentic Bengali thalis served in traditional Kashar tableware (featuring freshwater Katla, Bhetki Paturi, Golda Chingri, and Kosha Mangsho) along with North Indian, Tandoor, and popular Chinese favorites. Pure vegetarian thalis are also prepared with dedicated purity.

Can we host destination weddings, pre-wedding shoots, or corporate offsites?

Yes! Our expansive open-air celebration lawns can host over 1,000 guests, complete with indoor banquet facilities, stage setups, sound systems, lawn lighting, and full cottage buyouts for weddings and team offsites.
